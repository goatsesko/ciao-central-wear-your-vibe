# Ciao Central Wear Your Vibe

Build CIAO CENTRAL, a premium Kenyan streetwear and lifestyle brand store. Slogan: "MORE THAN CLOTHES. IT'S A LIFESTYLE." Clothing store is the priority; the architecture must later expand to Apps, Team, Story and Contact pages.



DESIGN: Editorial luxury fashion. Black, white, charcoal, subtle silver/gold accents. Bold condensed display font for headings plus a clean sans for body. Oversized imagery, generous whitespace, refined scroll/hover animations, a distinctive CC monogram logo. Mobile-first, fast, accessible (contrast, focus states, alt text). Not a generic ecommerce template.



HOMEPAGE: Announcement bar (free delivery threshold, M-Pesa accepted), sticky nav with logo, search, account and bag icons, full-bleed hero "WEAR YOUR VIBE" with SHOP THE COLLECTION and EXPLORE NEW DROPS, New Arrivals, Bestsellers, Signature Tees, Denim Collection, campaign gallery, brand story, newsletter signup, social links (TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, Threads, handle @ciaocentral; URLs editable later, never invented).



CATALOG: /shop with search, category nav, sorting (newest, price), filters (price, size, colour), responsive grid, skeleton loaders, empty and error states. Product page: image gallery, description, size and colour variant selectors, SKU, stock status (In stock / Low / Sold out), quantity selector, related products, SEO title and meta.



SEED DATA: clearly labelled "SAMPLE" products: black oversized CC T-shirt, white signature T-shirt, black denim overshirt, with placeholder images and sample KSh prices. No fake reviews, sales or inventory claims.



CART: slide-out bag drawer, add/remove, quantity change, persistent via localStorage, subtotal, delivery estimate, total. Format all prices as KSh.



Use clean reusable components and a data layer that can swap to Supabase in the next step. Add placeholder routes: /apps, /about, /team, /contact, /support (FAQ, delivery, returns, size guide). The attached photo is our logo
